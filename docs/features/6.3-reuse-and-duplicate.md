# 6.3 — Reuse and duplicate dances

Requirement: **H7** (a dance belongs to the school and can appear in more than one show, or be duplicated as a starting point).

Done when: One dance in two shows; duplicate a dance with its cast and figures.

Screens: `DanceCard` on Home (load the `living-stage-design` skill). The design has no Duplicate control yet; this plan adds one with the `copy` icon.

## Decisions

- **One dance in several shows needs nothing new.** 6.1 already allows it: `ActItem` is unique on (show, dance), and the picker hides only the dances already in that show. 6.3 checks it in the browser.
- **The copy is called "X (copy)"**: the title plus " (copy)", with the title cut so the whole fits in 100 characters. Copying a copy gives "X (copy) (copy)". The copy is the newest dance, so it comes first on Home.
- **The copy takes everything but the shows:** the cast in the same order (dancers, roles and open spots), every figure in order with its name, transition time and positions, the label and color modes, the guides and the duration. It starts in no show.
- **Duplicate is an icon button on each dance card**, next to Rename: the `copy` icon, labelled "Duplicate X", at least 44 px. One tap, no confirmation, since nothing is lost.
- **After duplicating, Home opens the copy's rename form** with the title focused, so the teacher can name it at once. Cancel keeps "X (copy)".
- **The copy is made on the server in one transaction**: the dance, its cast members, figures and positions are written together or not at all. New cast members and figures get their ids up front so positions can point at them, and each cast member keeps the original's `createdAt`, which is what orders the cast.

## API

Under `/api/schools/:schoolId`:

- `POST /dances/:danceId/copies` → 201 `{ dance: DanceSummary }`, the copy with its cast count and first-figure preview (404 for a dance of another school).

## Slices

1. ✅ The copy title in `packages/shared`, and the copy route with its transaction.
2. ✅ Home: Duplicate on each dance card, then the copy's rename form opens.
3. ✅ Check "Done when" in the browser at iPhone, iPad and desktop sizes, then mark H7 and 6.3. Checked on 5 Nov 2026 at 1194 × 834 (driven), 393 × 852 and 1440 × 900: Duplicate on a 40-dancer dance with 4 figures gave "Forty dancers (copy)" first on Home with its rename form open and focused; the copy had the same cast and roles in order and the same figure names, times and positions, and opened in the editor; added to two shows, it showed in both and the delete warning named both; no sideways scroll and every control at least 44 px.

## Out of scope

- Duplicating a whole show, copying a dance into another school, a Duplicate control inside the editor (its toolbar's Duplicate copies a figure).
