# 2.6 — Undo and redo

Requirements: **C12** (undo and redo while editing, kept per figure).

Done when: Undo/redo per figure.

Screens: `Toolbar` (load the `living-stage-design` skill).

Status: ✅ done on 24 Nov 2026.

## Decisions

- **One history per figure**, as agreed in §7: undoing on Figure 3 only touches Figure 3, and switching figures keeps each figure's history.
- **What can be undone:** what happens on the stage of one figure: moving dancers (one or many), bending a path, adding, moving or removing waypoints, and circling or straightening a path, and the figure's name, move time and moment in the song (typed in its panel or dragged on the waveform). Saving the panel unchanged is not a step.
- **What can't:** adding, duplicating, reordering and deleting figures, and cast changes. They act on the whole dance, not one figure, and deleting already asks first. Removing a dancer from the cast clears every figure's history, since older steps may hold that dancer.
- **An undo is a save.** It sends the step's earlier state through the routes that exist today (positions, paths and the figure itself), so nothing new is needed on the server. If the save fails, the step stays where it was, like a failed drag.
- **History lives in memory** for as long as the dance is open, up to 50 steps per figure. Leaving the dance or reloading clears it. A new edit clears the redo steps.
- **Undo and Redo are toolbar tools**, after Duplicate, disabled when there is nothing to undo or redo. Keys: Ctrl/Cmd+Z to undo, Shift+Ctrl/Cmd+Z or Ctrl+Y to redo; inside a text field they keep the field's own undo. They work only while playback is stopped, like editing.
- On iPhone they show icon-only, like the other tools.
