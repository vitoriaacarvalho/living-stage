# 6.5 — Show details and running time

Requirements: **H4** (each dance in a show displays its cast, how many dancers it has and its duration) and **H6** (the show's total running time comes from the dance durations; in the MVP the teacher types each one).

Done when: Each dance shows its cast, dancer count and typed duration; the show's total time is right.

Screens: `ShowManager` (running time header, act totals, interval), `DanceRow`, `ShowCard` (load the `living-stage-design` skill).

## Decisions

- **A dance's duration belongs to the dance**, so it is the same in every show (`Dance.durationMs`, empty until typed). Music will replace it once songs ship (E).
- **It is typed on the show's dance row.** The time at the end of the row is a button; tapping it opens a small field for `m:ss` (e.g. `3:40`), saved with Enter or Save and dropped with Escape or Cancel. Whole seconds from `0:01` to `60:00`; a blank field clears it. A dance with no time shows `–:––` and counts as nothing.
- **A show can have an interval between acts**, one length for every gap (`Show.intervalMs`, 0 by default), typed in whole minutes from 0 to 60 in the show's Edit form ("Interval between acts"). With two or more acts and an interval set, a dashed "Interval · 15:00" row sits between the act cards. No transition time between dances.
- **The running time** is the dances' durations plus the intervals. The show screen shows it at the top right as in the design: the overline "TOTAL RUNNING TIME", the total in the serif display style as `57min 15s`, a bar split into each act (rose, alternating `primary` and `primary-strong`) and each interval (`border-control`), and a legend `Act 1 14:58 · Interval 15:00 · Act 2 22:17`. When some dances have no time yet, a line under it says how many ("2 dances have no time yet").
- **Each act head shows its own total** (`14:58`, tabular numerals).
- **The cast is one tap away:** tapping a row's dancer count opens the names under the row (name, then role, in cast order; an open spot shows as "Open spot"); tapping again closes it.
- **Show cards on Home add the total** to their counts: `2 acts · 10 dances · 57min`.

## Data model

```
Dance   + durationMs?        whole seconds, 1 s to 60 min
Show    + intervalMs         whole minutes, 0 to 60 min, 0 by default
```

## API

- `PATCH /dances/:danceId` takes `durationMs` (or `null` to clear) besides the fields it already takes.
- `POST` and `PATCH /shows/:showId` take `intervalMs`.
- `GET /shows/:showId` gives each dance its `durationMs` and its cast (`{ id, name, role }`, `name` null for an open spot), and the show its `intervalMs`.
- `GET /shows` gives each show its `totalMs`.

2. ⬜ Show screen: durations typed on the rows, act totals, the interval rows, and the running time header.
3. ⬜ The cast under a row, on tap.

## Out of scope
