# 2.7 — Straight-line playback

Requirements: **D1** (a move between two figures is a straight line by default), **D4** (playback animates every dancer smoothly with easing), **D5** (playback runs without music, with a default duration per transition), **D6** (the teacher sets each move's time), and **E9** (up to 40 dancers per dance, rendering and animating smoothly on an iPad).

Done when: 40 dancers animate smoothly between figures without music on an iPad.

Screens: `Timeline` (only its transport row), `Stage` (load the `living-stage-design` skill).

## Decisions

- **Each move has its own time, 4 seconds by default** (`TRANSITION_MS` in `packages/shared`). It lives on the figure being reached as `transitionMs`, the time to arrive from the figure before, so it stays with the figure when figures are reordered and Duplicate copies it. The first figure's time is ignored. The teacher sets it in the figure panel as "Time to arrive", 1 to 30 seconds in half seconds; the first figure's panel doesn't show it. Figure times and counts come with music (E4) and replace it. Figure times and counts come with music (E4).
- **There is no hold at a figure.** Each transition eases in and out (ease-in-out cubic), so dancers slow into a figure and leave it smoothly. The motion reads as a pause without dead time.
- **A straight line needs no `Path` rows:** a dancer goes from her spot in one figure to her spot in the next. `Path` arrives with curves in 3.1.
- **The animation is one pure function in `packages/shared`**, as §5 of the plan says. `stageAt(figures, timeMs)` returns every cast member's spot, the figure being left, the one being reached and the progress between them. The plan draws from it.
- **Play starts at the selected figure** and runs to the last one, then stops there with it selected. From the last figure, Play starts again at the first.
- **Pause holds the in-between pose.** Play goes on from there, and tapping a figure chip stops playback and jumps to that figure. While playback runs or sits paused between figures, dragging and selection (2.4) are off.
- **The transport row** is the play and pause button with the time as `00:04 / 00:12` (tabular numerals), above the figure strip. This is the left end of the design's `Timeline` transport row. Speed, loop and the waveform are E5 and E2. While playing, the chip of the figure being reached is marked.
- **One clock, `requestAnimationFrame`**, from a playback hook. It starts out simple: the time is React state and the plan redraws each frame.
- **E9 decides the next step.** If 40 dancers with labels aren't smooth on an iPad, the cheaper fix comes first: the playback time leaves `DanceEditor`, so each frame re-renders only the stage, not the cast list, toolbar and figure strip. If that still isn't smooth, positions move to refs and the dots are moved directly each frame, with no React render per frame.
- **The measurement needs a real Apple device.** Use an iPad if possible; otherwise a recent iPhone is the closest stand-in. Desktop Chrome with 4× CPU slowdown is only a hint. Record the device and the numbers here.
- **A dance with one figure has nothing to play:** Play is disabled and the time reads `00:00 / 00:00`.
- **New figure and Duplicate stop playback first**, like tapping a chip, and the new figure is selected. The cast list stays usable during playback; the pose updates with it.
- **A dance holds at most 40 dancers (E9).** The cast route refuses the 41st with 409, and the cast picker stops offering dancers and says the cast is full.
- **Reduced motion doesn't turn playback off**, since watching the dancers move is what the teacher asked for. UI motion around it stays calm and short.
- **Tests:** `stageAt` is unit tested at its edges: time 0, exactly at a figure, halfway with easing, past the end, a single figure, and dancers coming in from or going out to the wings. The hook is tested with fake timers. The views are checked in the browser.
