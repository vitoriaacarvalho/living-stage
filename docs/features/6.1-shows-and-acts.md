# 6.1 — Shows and acts

Requirements: **H1** (a member creates a show with a name, date and optional venue) and **H2** (a show holds ordered acts; each act holds ordered dances).

Done when: Create a show with acts holding dances.

Screens: `ShowCard` (on Home), `ShowManager` (acts and their dances), `DanceRow` (load the `living-stage-design` skill).

## Decisions

- **A show has a title, a date, an optional time and an optional venue.** The date is a calendar day (`YYYY-MM-DD`) and the time a wall-clock time (`HH:MM`), both kept as typed, so a show at 7 pm stays at 7 pm whatever the device's time zone. Titles and venues are trimmed, up to 100 characters; blank venue and time mean none.
- **Acts are numbered, with an optional name**, like figures: "Act 1", "Act 2", or "Act 1 · Classics" once named (up to 40 characters). A new show starts with Act 1 so dances can be added right away. New acts go at the end. A show always keeps at least one act: deleting the last answers 409 `last_act`. Deleting an act takes its dances out of the show, never out of the school.
- **A dance is in a show at most once**, and can be in several shows (H7). Adding it again answers 409 `already_in_show`; the picker only offers dances not yet in the show. Dances go to the end of their act. Removing one closes the gap.
- **Reordering acts and dances by dragging is 6.2.** In 6.1 the order is the order they were added.
- **Deleting a show** asks first and removes its acts, never its dances.
- **Deleting a dance that is in shows** asks first and names them ("It is in Winter gala and Spring show and will leave them"), then takes it out of those acts. This settles the open question in §7 of the plan.
- **Home lists the shows above the dances**, as `ShowCard`s: a stage thumbnail of the first dance's first figure (an empty stage while the show has no dances), the title in the serif, the date and time, the venue, and the number of acts and dances. Upcoming shows come first, soonest first; past ones follow, most recent first.
- **"Performed" is the only status**, shown as an outline chip on a show whose date has passed. Draft and In rehearsal from the design are not in the plan.
- **New show is Home's one primary action**; New dance becomes secondary, as in the design. New show opens a small form (title, date, time, venue) in the Shows section, like New dance.
- **The show screen opens from its card**, inside the shell with Home marked, and has a back link to Home. It shows the title in the serif `display` style, the date, time and venue with an Edit button, then each act as a card listing its dances as `DanceRow`s (order number, title, dancer count) with Remove, and "Add dance to Act N" under them. The running time, the interval, songs, durations and costume thumbnails come with 6.5 and later.

## Data model

```
Show      id, schoolId, title, date (date), time?, venue?, createdAt
Act       id, showId, order, name?                 unique (showId, order)
ActItem   id, showId, actId, danceId, order        unique (actId, order), unique (showId, danceId)
```

`ActItem.showId` is there only so the database can refuse a dance twice in one show. Deleting a show, an act or a dance cascades to its items.

## API

All routes are under `/api/schools/:schoolId`:

- `GET /shows` → `{ shows: ShowSummary[] }`: each show with its act and dance counts and the preview spots of its first dance
- `POST /shows` `{ title, date, time?, venue? }` → 201 `{ show }`, created with Act 1
- `GET /shows/:showId` → `{ show: ShowDetail }`: the show with its acts in order, each with its dances (id, title, cast count)
- `PATCH /shows/:showId` `{ title?, date?, time?, venue? }` → `{ show }`
- `DELETE /shows/:showId` → 204
- `POST /shows/:showId/acts` → 201 `{ act }` at the end
- `PATCH /shows/:showId/acts/:actId` `{ name }` → `{ act }`
- `DELETE /shows/:showId/acts/:actId` → 204 (409 `last_act`)
- `POST /shows/:showId/acts/:actId/dances` `{ danceId }` → 201 `{ item }` (409 `already_in_show`, 400 for another school's dance)
- `DELETE /shows/:showId/acts/:actId/dances/:itemId` → 204

The dance list also sends, for each dance, the titles of the shows it is in, for the delete warning.
