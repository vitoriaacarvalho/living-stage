# 8.2 — iPhone editor

Requirements: **K3** (on iPhone, the editor is for viewing and quick edits; landscape is suggested for heavy editing). Closing it also closes **K1** and plan 1.6.

Done when: Quick edits work on iPhone; landscape hint for heavy editing.

Screens: `DanceEditorIPhone` (load the `living-stage-design` skill). The design also shows the editing banner (Part F), speed, loop and the time readout (Part E), and Mirror, Align and Spread (2.5). Those are after the MVP, so they are left out. The design has no landscape hint and doesn't say where the figure panel goes; both are decided below.

## Decisions

- **Phone means under 600 px wide**, the breakpoint the Stage already uses for its smaller dots. `usePhone()` reads it with `matchMedia`, so the editor can lay out differently, not just restyle.
- **Compact title bar.** Back, then the dance title over "Figure 3 of 7" (the selected figure's place), then Present as an icon. On the phone, Present leaves the figure toolbar.
- **Full-bleed stage.** The stage runs edge to edge on a `stage-wing` band and is sized by the screen's width, not the space left over.
- **The cast is a peeking bottom sheet**, as in the design: it sits under the timeline and drags up to expand. The figure panel takes the cast's place in the sheet, the same way it takes the sidebar on iPad.
- **Landscape hint.** On a phone in portrait, a slim note under the title bar: "Turn sideways for more room to edit", with an ✕ that hides it for good on that device (local storage).

## Slices

1. ✅ Phone title bar with the figure count and Present; full-bleed stage.
2. ✅ Compact controls: one view-controls row with icon-only Guides and Snap, and "F3" markers on the timeline.
3. ⬜ Bottom sheet: peeking, drag or tap to expand, holding the cast list or the figure panel.
4. ⬜ Landscape hint.
5. ⬜ Check "Done when" in the browser at 393 × 852 (and iPad and desktop for no regressions), then mark K3, K1, 8.2 and 1.6 ✅.

## Out of scope

- The editing banner, speed, loop, the time readout, Mirror, Align and Spread (after the MVP), and a "more" menu, which has nothing to hold yet.
