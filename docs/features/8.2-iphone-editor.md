# 8.2 — iPhone editor

Requirements: **K3** (on iPhone, the editor is for viewing and quick edits; landscape is suggested for heavy editing). Closing it also closes **K1** and plan 1.6.

Done when: Quick edits work on iPhone; landscape hint for heavy editing.

Screens: `DanceEditorIPhone` (load the `living-stage-design` skill). The design also shows the editing banner (Part F, dropped), speed, loop and the time readout (Part E, after the MVP), and Mirror, Align and Spread (dropped). They are left out. The design has no landscape hint and doesn't say where the figure panel goes; both are decided below.

## Decisions

- **A phone is a screen under 600 px wide (portrait) or under 500 px tall (landscape).** Both get the compact title bar, phone-sized dots, icon-only tools and toggles, and "F3" markers. `usePhone()` returns `'portrait'`, `'landscape'` or null, so the editor can lay out differently, not just restyle.
- **Compact title bar.** Back, then the dance title over "Figure 3 of 7" (the selected figure's place), then Present as an icon. On the phone, Present leaves the figure toolbar.
- **Full-bleed stage.** The stage runs edge to edge on a `stage-wing` band and is sized by the screen's width, not the space left over.
- **The cast is a peeking bottom sheet**, as in the design: it sits under the timeline and drags up to expand. The figure panel takes the cast's place in the sheet, the same way it takes the sidebar on iPad.
- **Landscape puts the stage beside the tools.** The stage fills the height on the left; a 380 px column on the right holds the view controls, the toolbar, the timeline and the cast sheet, which expands within that column so the stage stays in view.
- **Landscape hint.** On a phone in portrait, a slim note under the title bar: "Turn sideways for more room to edit", with an ✕ that hides it for good on that device (local storage).

Turned to 852 × 393, the stage is 472 × 313 beside the tools, a finger drag saved, and the sheet expands in its column. iPad (1194 × 834) and desktop (1440 × 900) look as before. No sideways or page scroll.
6. ✅ Landscape layout: the stage beside the tools.

Found and fixed in the check: segments and F markers were 42–43 px wide, and phone landscape showed no stage at all (the iPad layout's cast list took the whole 393 px height), which the hint would have sent people to.
