# 2.4 — Dragging and selection

Requirements: **C8** (dancers are placed by dragging in both views; several can be selected and moved together; in 3D the finger moves the ballerina's feet across the floor), **C9** (optional snapping to guide lines), and the selected ring of **L3**.

Done when: drag one or many dancers in the 2D plan and the 3D stage, with optional snapping, by finger and mouse.

Screens: `Stage`, `Stage3D` (load the `living-stage-design` skill, and for the 3D half the 3D skills listed in `CLAUDE.local.md`).

## Decisions

- **Tap a dancer to select her; tap the empty floor to clear.** Tapping another dancer replaces the selection. Esc also clears it.
- **Several dancers are selected with a box:** drag on the empty floor to draw it, and every dancer whose spot falls inside is selected. On desktop, Shift-click adds or removes one dancer. Both work in both views; in 3D the box is drawn on screen and tests each ballerina's feet.
- **Dragging a selected dancer moves the whole selection by the same amount.** Dragging a dancer who isn't selected selects only her first, then moves her. One drag at a time: a second finger is ignored.
- **The selection survives switching figures and views**, so a group can be picked once and placed in figure after figure. It is cleared when a selected dancer leaves the cast.
- **Positions stay inside the plan:** `x` from `-SIDE_WING_DEPTH` to `1 + SIDE_WING_DEPTH`, `y` from `-UPSTAGE_WING_DEPTH` to `1`. Nobody goes past the downstage edge into the audience. When a group hits the edge, the whole group stops, so its shape never squashes.
- **Snapping is a "Snap" toggle next to Guides, and works only while guides are shown**, because you can't aim at lines you can't see. The toggle is disabled when guides are hidden. It isn't saved. Within 0.25 m of the centre or a quarter line, the dragged dancer's `x` or `y` jumps onto it; the rest of a group keeps its offsets.
- **Saved on drop.** The new spots show at once and go back if saving fails, like reordering figures. Only the dancers that moved are sent.
- **Selected look:** a `stage-accent` ring on the floor in 3D (L3), and the design's selected ring around the dot in 2D (check the `Stage` preview).
- **2D drag uses Pointer Events** on each dot (the `dancer-hit` area of 56 px), with pointer capture and `touch-action: none` on the stage. Pointer position is turned into stage units through the plan's bounding box.
- **3D hit testing happens on screen, not by raycasting the meshes.** Each ballerina's body centre (about 0.9 m up) is projected to the screen. A press picks the nearest one within half of `dancer-hit` (28 px), frontmost first. That keeps the design's hit area centred on the body and avoids testing about 600 meshes.
- **In 3D the finger moves the feet across the floor:** the pointer's ray meets the floor plane (`y = 0`), and the offset between that point and the feet at press time is kept, so the ballerina doesn't jump under the finger. The camera stays fixed (9.1), so this stays predictable.
- **Keyboard, in the 2D plan:** each dot is focusable, the arrow keys move the selection 0.25 m, and Shift with an arrow moves it 1 m. Each move saves. This is the non-drag alternative, since dragging must not be the only way. The 3D view stays `role="img"`, with the 2D plan one tap away.
- **`@use-gesture/react` is not needed yet.** Plain Pointer Events cover one-finger drag and the selection box. Pinch-to-zoom and pan aren't in C8.
- **Tests:** the pure parts live in `packages/shared` (plan bounds, moving a group, snapping, parsing the update) and the route has its own tests. 2D dragging is tested in jsdom with pointer events and a mocked bounding box. The 3D hit test and floor projection are pure functions tested with fixed cameras; the feel of the drag is checked in the browser.

## API

Under `/api/schools/:schoolId/dances/:danceId`:

- `PUT /figures/:figureId/positions` `{ positions: [{ castMemberId, x, y }] }` → `{ figure }`. The 400 cases:
  - the body is bad
  - a number isn't finite or is outside the plan
  - a cast member repeats or isn't in this dance

  A figure from another dance gets 404. Only the listed positions change.

## Open questions

- Is a box drawn on the empty floor the right multi-select on touch, or would teachers rather have a "Select" mode where taps add dancers?
- Should Snap be saved per dance, like Guides?

## Out of scope

- Layout tools for a selection (C10), ghosts of the previous figure (C11), undo and redo (C12), pinch-to-zoom and pan, curved paths (3.1), and dragging during playback (2.7).
