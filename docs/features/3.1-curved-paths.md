# 3.1 — Curved paths

Requirement: **D2**, its curves half (a path can be bent into a curve by dragging a handle). Waypoints, the other half, are plan 3.2.

Done when: Drag handles to bend a path; playback follows it.

Screens: `Stage` (load the `living-stage-design` skill). The design draws a selected dancer's path from her faint spot in the figure before to her spot now, with an arrowhead and a handle. This plan follows it, except that the handle sits on the curve instead of off it (see Decisions).

## Decisions

- **A path is the route a dancer takes into a figure**, from her spot in the figure before. The first figure has none. A dancer who stays put, or who is missing from the figure before, has none either.
- **The handle sits on the curve.** The curve always passes through it, so the teacher drags the line itself. A straight path shows its handle at the middle of the line. 3.2 adds more of the same points (waypoints), so one model covers both.
- **The curve is smooth through its points.** Start, points and end are joined by a Catmull-Rom spline (centripetal, so it doesn't loop), turned into cubic Bézier segments as §5 of the plan says. With one point it is a single gentle arc.
- **A bend is saved relative to the straight line**, not as a spot on the stage: `along` is how far along the line (0 to 1) and `side` how far off it, as a share of the line's length (positive to the left as the dancer travels). So when either end moves, or figures are reordered, the bend stretches and turns with the line and keeps its shape. The maths is in metres, so a bend keeps its shape on the 12 × 8 m floor.
- **Only selected dancers show their paths.** Each one shows her faint dashed circle in the figure before and the path to her spot now, in her color, with an arrowhead, stopping short of both circles. With exactly one dancer selected, her path shows its handle. Nothing shows while playing or paused between figures.
- **The handle** is a ring in `stage-accent` with a 44 px (`touch-min`) hit area. A move shorter than 1.5 m shows its line but no handle, so the handle never sits on top of the dancer's own drag area. The handle is a button: "Bend the path of Ana", with the arrow keys moving it like a dancer (`ARROW_STEP_M`, Shift for the large step).
- **Snapping straight.** Letting go of the handle within 30 cm of the straight line removes the bend; the path is straight again. There is no Straighten button; undo is C12, after the MVP.
- **Playback follows the curve at the eased speed.** The easing is applied to the distance along the curve (an arc-length table of 32 samples), so a dancer on a curve slows in and out like one on a straight line.
- **Saving:** the handle moves on screen while dragged and is saved when let go, put back if saving fails, like dragging dancers.
- **Copies.** New figure and Duplicate copy no paths: the new figure starts with the same spots, so nobody moves into it. Duplicating a dance (6.3) copies every path.
- **Deleting.** Removing a dancer from the cast, or deleting a figure, deletes its paths. Giving an open spot to another dancer keeps them.

## Data model

```
Path   figureId, castMemberId, points[]   (points: [{ along, side }], one in 3.1; unique per figure and cast member)
```

`points` is JSON. In 3.1 there is exactly one point, `along` from 0.05 to 0.95 and `side` from −2 to 2.

## API

Under `/api/schools/:schoolId/dances/:danceId`:

- `GET /figures` → each figure also has `paths: [{ castMemberId, points }]`.
- `PUT /figures/:figureId/paths/:castMemberId` `{ points }` → `{ path }` (400 for the first figure or bad points, 404 for another school's dance or a cast member who isn't in it).
- `DELETE /figures/:figureId/paths/:castMemberId` → 204, the path is straight again.
