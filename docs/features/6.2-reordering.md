# 6.2 — Reordering

Requirements: **H3** (acts and dances can be reordered by dragging).

Done when: Drag acts and dances into a new order.

Screens: `ShowManager`, `DanceRow` (load the `living-stage-design` skill).

## Decisions

- **Acts and dances move by a grip handle**, as in the design's `DanceRow` and act heads: a 44 px tall handle with the grip icon at the start of each dance row and each act head. The rest of the row stays free for taps and scrolling.
- **The drag works like the figure strip (2.3):** `@dnd-kit/sortable`; a finger holds the handle for 250 ms before it lifts, so a quick swipe still scrolls; a mouse drags after 5 px. By keyboard, Space picks a handle up, the arrow keys move it and Space drops it; Escape puts it back. A screen reader hears where the item is now.
- **A dance can move into another act.** While it is dragged over another act it moves there, so the drop spot is always visible. Dropping it on an act's head or its empty space puts it at the end of that act.
- **An act carries its dances with it.** Acts only move among acts.
- **The lifted item follows the design:** a rose border and a soft shadow while dragged. Motion stays calm (dnd-kit's 150–200 ms ease), nothing bounces.
- **The new order shows on drop and goes back if saving fails**, with "Could not move it. Try again."
- **Saving sends the whole running order:** `PUT /shows/:showId/order` with every act in order and, for each, its dances' ids in order. The server checks that the acts and dances are exactly the show's own, each once, then rewrites the orders in one transaction, moving them out of the way first so the unique `(showId, order)` and `(actId, order)` never trip. Numbering closes any gap left by a deleted dance.

## API

- `PUT /api/schools/:schoolId/shows/:showId/order` `{ acts: [{ id, danceIds }] }` → `{ show: ShowDetail }` (400 unless the acts and dance ids are exactly the show's, 404 for another school's show). `danceIds` are the ids of the dances' places in the show (`ShowDance.id`).

## Slices

1. ✅ Shared: the order body and its check; the pure moves for acts and dances, with tests.
2. ✅ `PUT /shows/:showId/order`.
3. ⬜ Drag handles on acts and dances in the show screen, by finger, mouse and keyboard; the order saves and goes back on failure.
4. ⬜ Check "Done when" in the browser, then mark H3 and 6.2.

## Out of scope

- Durations, intervals and the running time (6.5), dragging dances in from the school's list.
