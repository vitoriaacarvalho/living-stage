# 2.9 — Metre grid and smart guides

Requirements: **C15** (a 1 m grid under the guide lines in the editor), **C16** (while dragging, a dancer lines up with the other dancers), **C17** (while dragging, a dancer snaps to an equal gap with her neighbours, in rows, columns and diagonals).

Done when: In the editor, Guides shows a 1 m grid; with Snap on, a dragged dancer lines up with another dancer or lands at an equal gap, with a line showing why, and lets go cleanly.

Screens: `Stage`, the editor's view controls (load the `living-stage-design` skill).

## Decisions

- **The grid is the editor's.** Guides shows thin lines every metre (11 across, 7 deep) under the centre and quarter lines, in `stage-line` and fainter than the quarters, since guides must never compete with dancers. Presentation mode and thumbnails keep the six guide lines only. The Stage's `showGuides: boolean` becomes `guides: 'none' | 'lines' | 'grid'`.
- **Snap drives everything.** One pill, no new setting. Snap on means: line up with dancers and equal gaps always, and, while Guides is on, the guide lines and the grid. Snap is no longer greyed out when Guides is off, because smart guides draw their own lines. It still isn't saved.
- **What wins.** An equal-gap spot first (it fixes both axes), then per axis: another dancer, then a centre or quarter line (0.25 m, as today), then a grid line (0.1 m, so the grid helps without making every spot sticky).
- **Lining up (C16).** Within 0.15 m of another dancer's `x` or `y` in metres, the dragged dancer takes it. A line in `stage-accent` runs from that dancer to her while the drag lasts.
- **Equal gaps (C17).** Worked out in metres so diagonals keep their shape. Only the nearest dancers count, so a large cast doesn't make the whole floor sticky:
  - **Next in the pattern:** B is the dancer nearest to the dragged one; for each of B's two nearest neighbours A, the spot B + (B − A). Covers rows, columns and diagonals with one rule.
  - **In between:** the midpoint of the two dancers nearest to the dragged one.
  - Within 0.15 m of a spot, she lands on it. A dashed `stage-accent` line runs through the three dancers, with the gap in metres ("1.5 m", `caption`, tabular numerals) on both halves.
- **Groups.** Only the dancer under the finger snaps; the rest of the group keeps its offsets, and the dancers moving with her are not targets.
- **Arrow keys don't snap**, as in 2.4.
- **Pure math in `packages/shared`** with tests: grid snapping, lining up and equal gaps return the snapped spot and the hints to draw. The Stage only draws the hints.
- **Design difference:** the design's Stage has no grid and no smart guide lines; the design should gain both.

## Slices

1. ⬜ Stage: `guides` prop, the 1 m grid in the editor.
2. ⬜ Shared: snapping to the grid, after the guide lines, only in `grid` mode, with tests.
3. ⬜ Shared: lining up with other dancers, with hints, with tests.
4. ⬜ Shared: equal gaps (next in the pattern and in between), with hints, with tests.
5. ⬜ Editor: the drag uses smart snapping; Snap works with Guides off.
6. ⬜ Stage: draw the hints while dragging.
7. ⬜ Check "Done when" in the browser at iPad, iPhone and desktop sizes, then mark C15–C17 and 2.9 ✅.

## Out of scope

- Snapping by the arrow keys, saving Snap per dance, buttons to spread or mirror a selection (layout tools were dropped in Nov 2026), and lining up whole groups by their edges.
