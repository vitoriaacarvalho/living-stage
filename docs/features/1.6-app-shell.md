# 1.6 — Responsive app shell

Requirements: **K1** (every screen works on iPad, iPhone and desktop) and **K2** (touch targets big enough for fingers), for the screens around the editor.

Done when: Navigation and layout work on iPhone, iPad and desktop, and Home matches the design's `Home` for the parts that exist.

Screens: `Home` (shell, nav rail, dance cards). Load the `living-stage-design` skill.

## Decisions

- **Only what exists is in the shell.** The rail has Home, Dancers and Team. The design's Shows, Dances page, Settings, search, songs, durations and "who is editing" come with their own plans (6.1, E, F) and stay out until then.
- **Rail on iPad and desktop, tab bar on iPhone.** From 768 px wide the rail is 232 px on the left, 72% `surface` so the page watermark shows through. Below that the same three destinations are a bottom tab bar, and a slim top bar carries the app name.
- **The account sits in one menu.** The rail foot shows the teacher's initials, name and school; on iPhone it is the last tab, with the initials only. Tapping it opens a small menu with the name and school, Switch school and Sign out.
- **Home** has the date as an overline and a greeting by the time of day ("Good afternoon, Marina"), then the Dances section. New dance stays the one primary action, in the section head, since there are no shows to add yet. The API status line leaves Home.
- **Dance cards follow the design's recent-dance card:** a stage thumbnail of the first figure on top, then the title, the number of dancers and the date added. Rename stays on the card.
- **The thumbnail is drawn from a summary the dance list sends:** the cast count and the first figure's spots, each with its color already chosen by the dance's color mode. It is its own small SVG (floor, wings, faint guides, dots with no labels), not the editor's `Stage`.
- **Dancers and Team open inside the shell.** Their own Back buttons go, since the rail or tab bar takes you back. The dance editor stays full screen, with its own back button.
