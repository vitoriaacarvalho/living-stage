# 2.3 — Figures

Requirements: **C7** (a dance is an ordered list of figures: add, duplicate, rename, reorder, delete).

Done when: add, duplicate, rename, reorder, delete figures.

Screens: `Toolbar`, `Timeline` (figure markers) (load the `living-stage-design` skill).

## Decisions

- **New figure copies the figure you're on and goes at the end. Duplicate copies it and goes right after it.** Dancers start where they were, so a new formation is a few moves away, not a full re-placement.
- **The toolbar has only New figure and Duplicate.** Mirror, Align, Spread and Circle are C10 (after the MVP), Path is D2, Previous is C11, Present is plan 7.x.
- **Figure markers are a strip of chips under the stage**, the marker row of the design's `Timeline` without the waveform and counts, which come with music (Part E). The selected figure's chip is `primary-soft`, and the stage shows that figure's positions.
- **An unnamed figure shows "Figure 3"**, numbered by its place in the order, so its label follows reordering. Names are trimmed, blank means no name, and the maximum is 40 characters.
- **Tapping the selected chip opens a figure panel in the sidebar**, in place of the cast list, like the cast member panel: Name, Move earlier / Move later, and Delete with confirmation.
- **Reordering sends the whole order:** `PUT /figures/order` with every figure id. The server rewrites the orders in one transaction, moving them out of the way first so the unique `(danceId, order)` never trips.
- **A dance always keeps at least one figure.** Deleting the last one answers 409 `last_figure`. After a delete the figure before it is selected (or the new first one), and the remaining orders close the gap.
- **Every figure has every cast member.** Casting a dancer gives her a wing spot (`nextWingSpot`) in every figure, each computed from that figure's own positions.

## API

All routes are under `/api/schools/:schoolId/dances/:danceId`:

- `GET /figures` → `{ figures: Figure[] }` (exists)
- `POST /figures` `{ copyOf, placement: 'end' | 'after' }` → 201 `{ figure }` (404 for another dance's figure)
- `PATCH /figures/:figureId` `{ name }` → `{ figure }`
- `PUT /figures/order` `{ figureIds }` → `{ figures }` (400 unless the ids are exactly this dance's figures)
- `DELETE /figures/:figureId` → 204 (409 `last_figure`)

## Slices

1. ⬜ `POST` cast puts the new dancer in the wing of every figure.
2. ⬜ `POST` figures: copy a figure to the end or right after it.
3. ⬜ Frontend: the cast hook keeps every figure and the selected one; the figure strip under the stage; the stage shows the selected figure.
4. ⬜ The toolbar with New figure and Duplicate.
5. ⬜ `PATCH` a figure's name, shared name validation, and the figure panel with Name.
6. ⬜ `PUT` figure order, and Move earlier / Move later in the panel.
7. ⬜ `DELETE` a figure (409 for the last one), and Delete with confirmation in the panel.
8. ⬜ Check "Done when" in the browser, then mark C7 ✅ and 2.3 ✅.

## Out of scope

- Dragging dancers (2.4), playback between figures (2.7), figure times and counts (E4, plan 4.5).
- Dragging chips to reorder.
