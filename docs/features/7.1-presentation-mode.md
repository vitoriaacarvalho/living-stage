# 7.1 — Presentation mode

Requirements: **G1** (full-screen stage with playback controls), **G2** (large, readable names for a TV or projector), **G3** (figure-by-figure stepping as well as continuous playback).

Done when: Full-screen stage readable on a TV; step or play continuously.

Screens: `PresentationMode`, and the `Toolbar`'s Present tool (load the `living-stage-design` skill). The design's presentation mode also has counts 1–8, speed, Repeat figure and a dark waveform timeline. Those need music (Part E, after the MVP), so they are left out and join with 4.3–4.5. Its Paths toggle isn't in the plan, so it is left out too.

## Decisions

- **Opened from the editor.** The figure toolbar's last tool, **Present** (the design's `present` icon, in `primary` like the design's accent tool), sits at the far end after a divider. It stops the editor's playback, asks the browser for full screen and shows presentation mode over the whole page, starting at the selected figure. Where the browser has no full screen (iPhone Safari), presentation mode still fills the page.
- **Left with the X button or Escape.** Leaving the browser's full screen leaves presentation mode too, so one Escape is enough. Back in the editor, the figure last shown is selected.
- **The ground is `stage-wing`, full screen.** At the top: the X button, then the kicker "Swans · Figure 3 of 7" (`overline`) over the figure's name in the serif (`display`, or "Figure 3" when it has none). On the right, "Next in 00:04" over "Figure 4 · Diagonal". The last figure has no Next line.
- **The current figure is the last one reached.** While a move plays, the header keeps the figure being left and the Next line counts down to the one being reached, in whole seconds rounded up.
- **The stage is as large as fits**, between the header and the controls, using the dance's own Guides, Labels (name, role or both) and Color by settings. Nothing is editable, and no paths or selection show.
- **Dots and labels grow with the stage.** Both scale with the stage's width: about 1.4× the editor at iPad size, larger on a TV, with the phone sizes as the floor (dot 5% of the plan's width, 26 px to 96 px; names 1.65%, 12 px to 32 px). The Stage reads two CSS variables with the editor sizes as defaults, so the editor doesn't change.
- **The controls are always shown**, in a row under the stage: Previous, a 64 px Play/Pause in `stage-accent`, Next, and the time as `00:04 / 00:12`. All are at least `touch-tool`.
- **Play** runs continuously from the current figure to the last, as in the editor (from the last figure it starts again at the first). **Pause** holds the pose.
- **Next plays the move into the next figure and stops there.** Paused or playing part way, Next goes on and stops at the figure being reached. Disabled at the last figure.
- **Previous jumps at once.** Stopped at a figure, it goes to the one before; part way through a move, back to the figure the move started from. Disabled at the first figure.
- **Keys:** Space plays and pauses, the right arrow is Next, the left arrow Previous, Escape leaves. Space or Enter on a focused button is left to the button.
- **One clock.** The playback hook gains `playTo(index)`, which plays to a figure and stops there; `play()` is `playTo` the last figure.

## Slices

1. ✅ Shared: `figureIndexAt(figures, timeMs)`, the last figure reached at a time, with tests. The playback hook's `playTo(index)`, with tests.
2. ✅ Presentation mode: the Present tool, full screen, the header, the large stage, Play/Pause and the time, leaving by X, Escape or the browser's full screen, back on the figure shown.
3. ✅ Stepping: Previous and Next, the arrow keys and Space, and the Next line with its countdown.
4. ✅ Check "Done when" in the browser at iPad, iPhone and desktop sizes, then mark G1–G3 ✅ and 7.1 ✅. Checked on 5 Nov 2026 at 1194 × 834, 393 × 852, 1440 × 900 and a 1920 × 1080 TV (driven): six dancers with names and roles, three figures (a line, a diagonal, a circle). Present opened the browser's full screen at the selected figure, with "Little swans · Figure 1 of 3", "Swans in a line" and "Next in 00:04 · Figure 2 · Diagonal". Dots and names against the editor's 40 px and 12 px: 51 px and 16.7 px on the iPad, 56 px and 18.5 px on desktop, 71 px and 23.4 px on the TV, and the phone floor of 26 px and 12 px on the iPhone. Next played the move with the countdown at 00:02 and stopped on Diagonal; Previous jumped back at once and was then off on the first figure; Play ran on, Pause held the dancers mid-move at 00:05 / 00:06, and at the end Next was off and the Next line gone. Left arrow, Space twice and right arrow stepped, played, paused and stepped. Leave presentation left full screen and the editor came back on Circle, the figure last shown; Escape left too. No errors, no sideways or page scroll. On the iPhone in portrait the stage is a strip with crowded names, as in the editor (8.2); a TV's header and time stay at their iPad sizes.

Found and fixed in the check: names came out at 1.2× on the iPad, so the scale went from 1.45% to 1.65% of the plan's width (dots from 4.5% to 5%); the kicker broke inside "Figure 2 of 3" on the iPhone.

## Out of scope

- Counts, speed, loop and the music timeline (Part E, joining in 4.3–4.5), showing paths, hiding the controls while playing, a separate window or link for a second screen.
